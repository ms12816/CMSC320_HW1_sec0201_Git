
Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: Git is a very useful tool for coding and development especially when working with teams. It allows you to
work together on different features at the same time through branching and version control. It is an industry
standard across the software engineer and computer science space, while also supporting individual project storage
for students and proffesionals.

Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: Data science is a vast and diverse field especially in the modern era where everything relies on technology
and analytics. One example is in health, where analyzing large data sets can help identify risks, forecast patient 
illness, and help map symptoms to treatments
